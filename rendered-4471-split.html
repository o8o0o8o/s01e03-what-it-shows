The problems

  1. Parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get day and month swapped. Fix: parse with the tenant's locale.
  2. Form hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants' people type month first, and the form reads it day first. The parser fix does not touch this. Fix: print the hint from the tenant's locale.
  3. One-day move. Some dates move by one day, not by a swap: the same meeting lands on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. That is a time zone bug. Fix: store and send dates with their zone.
